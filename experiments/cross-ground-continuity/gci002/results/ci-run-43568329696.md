## Evidence and trust limits

This establishes **finite observational congruence and certificate-bound
navigation under the exact declared intention**, not universal semantic
identity across arbitrary observers.

- E-CPU's eight observations are *mathematical source calculations* under a
  pinned software module, not measurements of physical devices.
- VGI typed evidence uses explicitly **synthetic message/frame identifiers**;
  those identifiers are not authenticated transport receipts. One-source
  quorum only authorizes a policy-relative experimental Ground.
- The closed certificate covers the eight-state quotient; the 16-bit result
  cannot be recovered from that quotient alone.
- Independent jobs share GitHub's administrative authority; a GitHub workflow
  attestation verifies declared provenance and does not prove process memory
  contents or physically independent hosts.
- GitHub's main branch metadata reports `protected: false`. Main-workflow
  signer restrictions do not establish branch protection.
- No Lean/kernel theorem or physical/network/energy benefit was produced.
