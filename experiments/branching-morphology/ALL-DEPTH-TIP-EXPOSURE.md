# BMO-009 — The nested branch tip is exposed at every finite depth

**Date:** 2026-11-14. **Scope:** convex hull of endpoints of the fixed grammar `F -> F[+F]F[-F]F` with unit segment steps, initially upward, and angle `0 < θ < π/2`. This is independent mathematical geometry, not a statement about internal line intersections or living plants.

The earlier [all-depth stabilization work](ALL-DEPTH-STABILIZATION.md) and [depth-six quintic certificate](EXACT-DEPTH6-QUINTIC.md) established that known hull transitions persist. This follow-up asks a different question: **must the recursively nested branch tip eventually become a genuine exposed hull vertex at every depth, rather than remain a merely internal collinearity?**

## Theorem: a quantitative, all-depth supporting edge

Write `O` for the origin and `A_n` for turtle endpoint number `a_n=(3·5^n+1)/4` after depth `n≥1`. Let `H_n(θ)` be the convex hull of *all* endpoints of the drawing.

**For every finite depth `n≥1`, the oriented segment `O→A_n` is a strict supporting edge of `H_n(θ)` whenever**

```text
0 < θ² <= 1 / (4 * n³ * 3^n),  θ in radians.
```

The proof gives the stronger quantitative guarantee that, for **every other endpoint** `p`,

```text
cross(O, A_n, p) >= 3^n * θ / 16 > 0.
```

Consequently, `A_n` is a real exposed convex-hull vertex on a **nonempty angular interval at every finite depth**, not just depths two through six. This does **not** certify exposure all the way to the previously computed candidate angle `θ_n`, nor prove exactly `n` hull transitions at depth `n`.

## 1. Two integer moments turn the tree into a cone

An endpoint is reached by a path of unit steps at relative integer headings `kθ`. Define `T` as the total number of steps and `K` as the **negative sum of integer headings** along that path. Then

```text
x(θ) = Σ sin(kθ) = -θ K + O(θ³)
y(θ) = -Σ cos(kθ) = -T + O(θ²).
```

The origin has `(T,K)=(0,0)`. The grammar's depth-`m+1` drawing is exactly five affine copies of its depth-`m` drawing. With `s=3^m`, the corresponding **exact integer-moment transformations** are:

| Copy | Moment transformation |
| --- | --- |
| 0, initial | `(T,K)` |
| 1, right rotation after trunk | `(s+T,K−T)` |
| 2, straight after trunk | `(s+T,K)` |
| 3, left rotation after two trunks | `(2s+T,K+T)` |
| 4, final straight | `(2s+T,K)` |

Every old point satisfies `0≤T≤s`; hence every new point satisfies `0≤T≤3s`.

Define the integer **slack** `S=T−1−2K` for non-origin points. The respective transformed slacks are exactly

```text
S,  S+s+2T,  S+s,  S+2(s−T),  S+2s.
```

If the old point has `S≥0`, all five are nonnegative. Copying the origin requires a separate check: its new non-origin copies have slack `s−1,s−1,2s−1,2s−1`, also nonnegative for `s≥1`. At depth zero, the sole non-origin point has `(T,K)=(1,0)` and `S=0`. Induction therefore proves:

```text
For every non-origin endpoint at every finite depth n:
      1 <= T <= 3^n,    S=T−1−2K >= 0.
```

## 2. The equality cases are exactly the nested tips

The same five slack transformations classify `S=0`. The first copy retains old equality points. Copies 1, 2, 4 from non-origin points have strictly positive new slack. The left-rotated copy 3 has zero slack **only** when the old point has `S=0` and `T=s`. Origin copies are strictly positive except the duplicate `(1,0)` at the first expansion.

Therefore the *distinct equality moments* at depth `n` are exactly

```text
(T,K) = (3^j, (3^j−1)/2),  j=0,...,n.
```

Their maximal-step member is uniquely generated by taking the left-rotated nested branch in each generation. Its first-seen endpoint number obeys `a_1=4` and `a_(n+1)=5a_n−1`, yielding

```text
a_n = (3·5^n+1)/4
T(A_n)=3^n
K(A_n)=(3^n−1)/2.
```

Thus this particular endpoint is not selected by a numerical hull algorithm. It is identified by a **unique extremal integer invariant**, valid at every depth.

## 3. The exact first-order hull margin is sharp

Let `s=3^n` and `K_A=(s−1)/2`. For any endpoint `p=(T,K)` other than `O,A_n`, define

```text
D_n(p) = K_A*T − s*K
       = ((s−T)+s*S)/2.
```

When `S≥1`, `D_n(p)≥s/2`. When `S=0` but `p≠A_n`, its step count is one of the smaller powers of three, so `T≤s/3` and `D_n(p)≥s/3`. The bound is **sharp**: the embedded previous nested tip has `T=s/3,S=0`, giving equality.

Hence

```text
D_n(p) >= s/3,
lim_(θ→0+) cross(O,A_n,p)/θ = D_n(p).
```

This establishes a strictly positive first derivative of the supporting-line inequality at angle zero, uniformly over all other endpoints of a given finite tree.

## 4. Prove an actual positive-angle interval

At depth `n`, every step's integer heading satisfies `|k|≤n`. By the elementary inequalities `|sin u−u|≤|u|³/6` and `|cos u−1|≤u²/2`, an endpoint with `T` steps obeys

```text
|x(θ)+θK| <= T*n³*θ³/6,
|y(θ)+T|  <= T*n²*θ²/2,
|x(θ)|    <= T*n*θ,
|y(θ)|    <= T.
```

For `A_n`, `T_A=s` and `|K_A|<s/2`. Expanding the determinant and applying the inequalities gives

```text
|cross(O,A_n,p) − θ D_n(p)|
  <= (13/12)*s*T*n³*θ³
  <= (13/12)*s²*n³*θ³.
```

For any `0<θ²≤1/(4n³s)`, the error is at most `(13/48)sθ`; the exact first-order gap is at least `(1/3)sθ=(16/48)sθ`. Subtracting,

```text
cross(O,A_n,p) >= (16/48−13/48)*sθ
                  = sθ/16 > 0.
```

Every other endpoint lies strictly on the left side of `O→A_n`, which proves the **all-depth supporting-edge and exposed-vertex theorem**.

This is a mathematical induction plus explicit trigonometric estimates, not an extrapolation of depths two through six.

## The remaining frontier

The explicit interval is conservative: `θ≤1/(2√(n³3^n))` radians is much smaller than the first collinearity root `θ_n` from BMO-007. We have proven that `A_n` **is exposed somewhere** for every depth. We have *not* proved that it stays exposed until its predicted polynomial root, that the root is the only new transition, or that all depths have exactly `n` transitions.

A promising next step is to replace the limiting integer-moment cone with an **angle-dependent invariant support cone**. If that cone can survive the five affine copies up to `θ_n`, it could establish root exposure and the true all-depth hull recurrence directly, without separate high-degree algebraic certificates.
