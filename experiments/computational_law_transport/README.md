# CLT-001 — Computational law transport

**Experiment:** discover an exact observer-conditioned chart between heterogeneous finite realizers, then use a previously formed law to form a new law without rereading the full source. This is a bounded finite experiment, not pretrained-model alignment.

## Construction

Three realizers have 64, 40 and 24 states respectively. A hidden three-bit coordinate evolves under increment, a nonlinear Boolean fold, and modular squaring plus one; unrelated nuisance registers and shuffled source names change the raw state spaces. The middle realizer actually executes E-CPU's two different 10,000-parameter ReLU gate architectures for AND and XOR when observing its fold table. Its remaining dynamics are ordinary Python. The native E-CPU INTENT-001 compiler and independent pair-distinguishability verifier form minimal future-observation machines from the full observed tables.

For the zero-marker intention, the quotient has eight classes. For parity, it has two. The increment exposes every distinct hidden coordinate by a future marker; parity is closed under all three actions. Chart discovery compares reachable product pairs of the minimal machines without paired source labels, then independently checks every output and action commutation. It rejects ambiguous or nonbijective charts. This is a known finite automaton equivalence construction; the experimental contribution is its integration into a source-qualified law transport and recursive reuse pipeline.

A second construction computes the parity of the distance to the marker by traversing the eight-state law. For this fixture the even period makes that derived quantity equal the original parity. This forms a two-state law entirely from the earlier eight-state marker quotient. A source-backed independent comparison checks the new law against direct native formation. The reverse direction requires at least two additional binary observations because each parity class contains four marker classes. Exact subset search over offered source upper-bit observations obtains both bits and reconstructs the marker quotient without a full source-state anchor. This is a scoped witness plan, not a general minimum-measurement oracle.

Repeated action words are composed and powered on both original and reduced tables; an independent raw-source cycle oracle checks the result up to a trillion repetitions. Counts include the word-map construction, each table squaring and each application; exclude initial source discovery, formation, verification, and transport. These are table reads, not latency or end-to-end cost savings. For small state spaces the cycle oracle may be cheaper than either powering route. Native INTENT-001 path realization also exercises loss of the preferred compact interpreter with the upstream fixture inventory. It is local carrier execution, not a remote network-host claim.

## Verification

- Complete native quotient verification, cross-chart commutation, inverse/composition and source-coordinate comparisons held out of chart construction.
- All source states and words through length four: 128 * 121 = 15,488 source versus native machine observations.
- Twenty fresh shuffled source realizers, nonlinear transition corruption, forged chart, recursive formation and minimal witness recovery.
- Long repeated words compared against original-source cycle replay and same-algorithm original-table powering.
- Native receiver-admitted route and replacement route retain machine identity and observation.
