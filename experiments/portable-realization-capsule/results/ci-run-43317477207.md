## Capsule semantics and integrity

The capsule binds source repository/revision, source Ground and typed program digests, dense/sparse model digests, compiler route, interface, codec schema, exact E-CPU/VGI/IFS pins, and a complete-domain replay receipt. Its framing digest, header digest and payload digest are checked independently.

SHA-256 provides integrity/identity checks, not author authentication or authorization. The verification receipt is replayable experiment evidence, not a formal proof certificate. The capsule does not yet integrate with E-CPU transport, admission, durable storage, or remote execution.
