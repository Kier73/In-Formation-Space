# MMG-007 — Positive-cost constraint, bottom-up quadratic grounds

## Intention

Select an exact bilinear realization of AB for A in a declared linear unital operator space S and arbitrary dense B. Admit only n² nonscalar multiplications (the output-rank floor) and at most 2 n² log₂ n + n log₂ n + 2 n² total field operations for the scalable Walsh family. The exponent is 2 in the usual infimum/slack definition, not strict O(n²) total work. This is a restricted starting problem, not a claim about arbitrary dense A.

## The cost constraint determines structure

Let S contain I. For any rank-n² bilinear decomposition of multiplication S × Mat_n → Mat_n, write L_A = C diag(f_i(A)) V, where V maps vectorized B to the n² linear forms, and C maps n² products to the output. Because L_I = I_(n²), both C and V are invertible and every f_i(I) is nonzero. Hence L_A = C diag(f_i(A)/f_i(I)) C⁻¹. All L_A commute and are simultaneously diagonalizable over the field. In particular S is commutative since L_A L_D = L_(AD). Each A is diagonalizable because L_A consists of n independent copies of A after reindexing. A commuting family of split diagonalizable matrices has a simultaneous eigenbasis over the field (successively restrict to invariant eigenspaces). Conversely, a simultaneous eigenbasis P gives AB=P diag(λ(A)) P⁻¹ B with exactly n² nonscalar products. The rank lower bound n² follows from the invertible identity slice. Thus within this exact minimal-rank class the positive constraint itself selects simultaneous diagonalization. It does not by itself give fast basis transforms, nor forbid O(n² polylog n) algorithms with larger rank.

At n=2 enumerate every two-dimensional linear subspace containing I: normalize a nonzero triple (b,c,d) to represent span(I, [[0,b],[c,d]]). Count eigenlines over F_p and construct each accepted common eigenbasis. There are p²+p+1 such spaces; split semisimple p(p+1)/2, repeated nonsemisimple p+1, irreducible p(p-1)/2. Check all coefficient assignments and right-input basis matrices; bilinearity extends to arbitrary right inputs. Non-split and repeated-eigenvalue non-scalar spaces cannot meet the exact rank floor over the same field. No reliance on sampled matrix equality for this classification proof.

## Scalable ground discovery

Input: k hidden permutation generators on n=2^k physical positions (arbitrary relabeling), a vector a of coefficients indexed by orbit points from physical anchor zero, arbitrary dense B. Establish group coordinates by traversing a spanning hypercube tree and verifying every labeled edge: each generator maps coordinate x to x XOR 2^j. Reject dependent or malformed generators; characteristic two lacks the invertible Walsh transform and is rejected by this ground. Define A[i,j] = a[loc[coord(i) XOR coord(j)]]. A is a linear combination of regular group shifts. If supplied dense, validate every entry against this declared source; one changed coefficient is rejected.

Over odd characteristic use characters χ_s(x)=(-1)^{s·x}. They diagonalize every shift simultaneously. The fast Walsh transform H factors into k butterfly stages, H²=nI. Compute H a, H B, pointwise products on n² entries, and n⁻¹ H of the result. Counts: n² nonscalar multiplications, (2n²+n)k additions/subtractions, ≤n² scalar normalization multiplications. Coordinate discovery costs O(nk) permutation observations; dense source admission O(n²) comparisons. These are separately reported; permutation/reindexing and data movement are not field arithmetic. The fast transform is valid over fixed odd fields, not a field whose size grows with n.

The JS experiment derives the coordinate map from permuted generators, not a hidden label oracle. Compare all output coordinates through n=128, and 512 independent-coordinate direct evaluations for n=256,512,1024; exact modular arithmetic. This is a local serial run. Full formal correctness follows from the character identity, not large-case sampling. The 2×2 enumeration, integer cost admission and malformed-input rejection are reproducible with node mmg007_positive_constraint.js. No E-CPU remote execution in this run.
