## MMG-030 — Exact collective metric on the residual quotient

Fix a rank-one prefix catalogue P, with pair span K. Existing prefix slices must continue to be realized using P. Let Q be the quotient map modulo K, and let s,t be the images of two newly revealed slices. Define d(x) to be the smallest number of rank-one atoms whose projected sum is x, equivalently the minimum matrix rank of a representative of the coset x. This is the shortest-path metric from zero in the binary additive quotient graph generated by the images of all rank-one atoms. For two output observations over GF(2), every nonzero output coefficient is one of 10,01,11. Group the new product terms by these three coefficients. If the shared 11 group has projected sum x, the other two projected sums must be s+x and t+x. Thus the exact minimum number of new products, conditional on retaining the prefix and not using the new terms for prior outputs, is

J(s,t)=min_x [d(x)+d(s+x)+d(t+x)].

The inequality J≤d(s)+d(t) follows by taking x=0. A lower bound is ceil((d(s)+d(t)+d(s+t))/2) by the three triangle inequalities. This is a three-terminal metric median problem, not a search over complete decompositions. A BFS on a finite quotient gives d and paths; independent Gaussian elimination reconstructs prefix coefficient corrections. The binary alphabet is important to the three-group formula; larger fields and more observations require more coefficient groups.

For T2, independently enumerate the three unordered minimum two-atom realizations of each rank-one output entry, then all nine pair choices for each of three prefix types. Quotient dimension 12 (4096 states), all 225 nonzero rank-one pair atoms before projection. Total 27 topologies, 110,592 reached quotient states, 17,952,768 edge checks. All three residual distances d(s),d(t),d(s+t) equal two. Same-row and same-column prefixes: J=4 for every topology. Diagonal prefix: J=3 in six of nine topologies and J=4 in three. The successful six reach total rank7 from two individually minimal outputs with four products. All 27 complete reconstructed tensors verified. The search is seeded with rank-one factorizations of isolated slices, not a rank7 decomposition.

First successful prefix queries [C11,C22], atoms [1,1],[2,4],[4,10],[12,8] in row-major masks. Reveal C12,C21 together. The exact metric median is 1784 in that quotient's coordinate system, with all three distances one. Resulting full witness [1,1,3],[2,4,5],[4,10,12],[12,8,10],[14,11,6],[15,3,2],[10,15,4]. The two remaining observations individually cost two new products, collectively three.

## MMG-031 — Scale the shared ground through the inner dimension

For T(2,k,2) reveal the two diagonal outputs first. Each individually has rank k, and their simultaneous flattening requires 2k products. Choose minimal prefix realizations whose rank-one atoms are confined to the respective diagonal blocks of the pair matrix. The residual off-diagonal queries s,t and their sum each contain an off-diagonal identity block I_k unaffected by K, hence d(s),d(t),d(s+t)≥k. The triangle bound proves J≥ceil(3k/2), and the fixed-prefix model requires at least 2k+ceil(3k/2)=ceil(7k/2) total products.

Tile the recovered T2 ground along inner-coordinate pairs, append a rank4 outer-product for an odd remaining coordinate. This attains that conditional bound. Apply seeded simultaneous inverse-transpose inner basis changes to scramble the block coordinates; exact coefficient verification confirms the algorithm remains valid and the diagonal prefix still uses 2k products. Test k=1..12,16,24,32,64,128. All 17 full tensor identities verified, plus 2,176 dense-input trials (128 per k) checked against classical multiplication. At k=128: 256 prefix products, 192 additional collective products, 448 total versus 512 classical. This construction is a direct sum of recovered small algorithms and is not a new rectangular rank bound. The conditional bound concerns the stated minimum-diagonal-prefix model, not unrestricted tensor rank. The scrambled coefficient masks may be dense; the experiment does not claim their translation cost is quadratic or report a scalar-time speedup.

## MMG-032 — Collective lift reaches rank23 from the rank24 witness

For one free leg of the rank24 witness, let K be the span of its 24 independent pair atoms and S the target slice space. Enumerate all (2^9−1)^2=261,121 rank-one atoms in the ambient pair space, group by their exact quotient Q modulo K. For a nonzero quotient class, pick a shared anchor a0. Differences a_i+a0 belong to K; m outside atoms can provide up to m−1 independent directions inside K for the cost of only one additional dimension.

Choose two independent dual constraints z,w of support≤4 that annihilate S. Their intersection H inside K has dimension 22. For an outside class, partition its atoms by the two parity values of their K coordinates under z,w. Differences within one parity bin lie in H. The inside atoms of H together with those outside atoms generate a subspace of dimension≤23 in K+span(a0), since the outside coordinate costs one dimension. If the inside atoms and same-bin differences span S, solve all target coefficients in this lifted subspace; emit actual rank-one products and independently verify the full tensor. This is not merely a quotient-space identity: the outside anchor coefficients cancel exactly in every original slice.

Axis0: 2,346 dual pairs, 970,470 group checks, 4,217,057 atom tests, no success. Axis1: success after 387 dual pairs, 254,916 group checks, 1,216,932 atom tests. The selected constraints (binary coordinate masks 33860,540704) left two independent residual directions; a quotient class of six atoms supplied them using a common anchor plus two more outside atoms. Twenty independent inside atoms and three outside atoms realize the entire T3 tensor with rank23. The original rank24 seed had been certified closed in each fixed pair space (MMG-021). The collective lift changed the space, so there is no contradiction. The previous single-difference lift MMG-023 had failed. Exact witness saved in mmg032_results.json. This independently recovers the established 23-product construction count; not a claim of originality or a smaller best-known tensor rank. The third axis is not searched after success.

## MMG-033 — Published rank47 direct challenge

A rank-two difference has three unordered two-rank-one decompositions over GF(2); enumerate them, project their atoms, and group matching nonzero quotient values. This gives a restricted catalogue of potentially valuable outside classes without enumerating all rank-one matrices. Each factorization is checked. ## MMG-034 — Independent verification and total cost of rank23

Reuse the MMG-011 compiler of shared XOR circuits, with exact transpose reconstruction and tensor powers. Independent verification evaluates every one of 81 pairs of matrix-unit inputs, comparing all nine outputs. Both rank24 and new rank23 witnesses pass; deliberately changed output coefficient rejected. Optimize each using the same common-subexpression search and available rank-preserving flip transformations. Rank24 optimized additions=62 (102 unshared, 63 initial shared), rank23=74 (121 unshared). The rank23 ground has no repeated single-leg factors and this flip-only optimization has zero applicable moves; record this, do not count attempted flips that were not performed. Further gauge and linear-circuit optimization remains possible.

Compile tensor powers k=1..4, all dense outputs verified. Rank23 at side81 has 279,841 products and 1,444,480 additions =1,724,321 field operations. Rank24 has 331,776 products and 1,344,222 additions=1,675,998. Thus rank23 has lower product count and lower asymptotic exponent bound log_3(23), but at this tested size the rank24 realization has lower total arithmetic. Classical dense multiplication at n=81 also uses fewer total field operations than either. This is why rank and complete operational cost must be tracked separately. The count excludes copying, memory and search/build work; timings and E-CPU distributed execution not measured. Every retained output matches the independent oracle.

## LGFC-001 v0.4 (proposed): collective continuation

Let task flag L_i grow by an observation subspace Δ_i instead of necessarily a single independent query. For each retained ground K, define joint marginal cost J_K(Δ) as the minimum number of new atoms realizing the projected residual subspace with the permitted old-atom reuse. A cheap individual query need not be the best next disclosure. Compare joint cost to the sum of separate costs, and use the gap as a signal of hidden shared structure. MMG-030 gives an exact example with J_K=3<2+2. MMG-032 shows the analogous phenomenon for a lift, where two missing directions are generated at the price of three outside atoms rather than two separate two-atom repairs. The task-depth policy should seek collections with high collective saving before fitting their individual residuals. The general finite-work advantage remains conjectural, and the stronger move does not remove all barriers (MMG-033).

Next: generate quotient groups using low-rank differences with support larger than four without brute enumeration, then choose multiple independent external quotient dimensions and reconstruct all residuals together; apply against the rank47/95 incumbents and rectangular targets. Exact identity and the below-9/4 exponent gate remain independent. All five experiments ran in the JS execution runtime and are source/result pinned.
