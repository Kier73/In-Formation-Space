# MMG-024–029 — Task-depth continuation and entangled reveal topology

## Intention and LGFC-001 extension

Keep the final unrestricted matrix multiplication tensor over GF(2) and its formal verifier. Let a task flag be a nested sequence of subspaces of the final output dual space, 0=L_0⊂L_1⊂...⊂L_m=F^(n²). At depth i, the task is to realize all output linear functionals in L_i for arbitrary independent input matrices. A full flag recovers every original output by an invertible linear decoding; its cost must be charged for any total-arithmetic claim. Alternatively, reveal nested independent input coordinate subsets to obtain rectangular multiplication. The final exponent target remains below 9/4; <2 is an infeasibility control for explicit scalar outputs.

Proposed refinement LGFC-001 v0.3 (task-depth conjecture): jointly selecting task flags, supporting spaces, move libraries and fitting pressure may increase first-hit probability at a fixed charged search budget, particularly when the flag exposes a low-cost initial realization and delayed commitments leave accessible cross-depth compression paths. The conjecture requires comparison against matched-work full-task search; the present experiments establish exact constructions and obstructions, not general comparative dominance. Do not count an easier prefix as a solution of the final task.

Define depth cost profile r_i as realized bilinear product count at each independent prefix, and integrated profile sum_i r_i. This is a descriptive measure of the curve, not search work, full algorithm cost, or a sum of execution savings when prefixes are not actually run. Different representations may be chosen at different depths. A retained-space continuation separately tracks whether the same rank-one atoms are carried across depth.

## MMG-024 — all coordinate-output subsets

Project our verified rank24 T3 and pinned rank47 T4 onto any nonempty output-coordinate subset. Compare fixed projection, all row/column permutation images, independent classical products, and a subset dynamic program composing disjoint output subproblems. Formal identity of every retained constituent follows by projection, permutation and disjoint output sum. The DP optimizes only this library and does not claim globally minimum tensor rank. A simple flattening lower bound is max(n*number of active rows, n*number of active columns, number of requested coordinates). For the n=4 witness mask 5184 (zero-based bit positions 6,10,12), fixed incumbent rank is 40 and a classical restricted realization has rank12, matching the flattening floor. The masks represent the same three output entries, not an alternative easier identity.

## MMG-026 — alternate linear output query topology

Let q be an n×n coefficient matrix for an output functional. Its slice matrix between vectorized A and B is, up to reindexing, q⊗I_n, of rank n*rank(q). Thus its isolated optimal bilinear rank is exactly n*rank(q); a matrix rank factorization attains this. For a fixed full witness, its output coefficient map encodes q into a support over the r product terms. Query cost from that witness is the weight of the support. For support U, all latent queries are exactly those codewords supported in U; find an independent basis. Enumerate all 511 and 65,535 nonzero query vectors for T3 and T4. Greedily choose a new query with minimum additional support (prefer lower query rank on ties), compare direct rank-factorized query realizations with projected incumbent. The chosen independent basis is full at final depth. Integrated rank profile: n=3 127 vs 132 for fixed coordinate order using the same two realization choices; n=4 470 vs 489. Fixed-incumbent profiles 163 and 540 respectively. Initial rank3 and 4 attain exact lower bounds. No new final rank improvement.

Why no extra latent dimensions were revealed in a greedy step: over GF(2), let U be current support and let V be the minimum nonempty new support of a query outside its closure. Any query in the expanded closure has additional support contained in V. By minimality this additional support is either empty or V. The quotient of the new query closure by the old one therefore has dimension exactly one. A multi-query shortcut must choose support non-greedily or change its ground. This proof is specific to binary support-minimizing disclosure.

## MMG-027 — actual carry-forward continuation

Let K be the span of retained rank-one matrices for earlier queries and M(q) the next slice. Find a low-rank representative of M(q)+K, factor it into rank-one matrices, add those atoms, and solve all prior and current coefficients together. Exact exhaustive combinations for at most ten retained atoms; otherwise bounded one/two-atom coordinate descent with seeded restarts. Random invertible rank-factorization basis changes explore alternate pair spaces. Candidate identities are verified at every depth; after all queries, invert the query basis and check the original dense tensor. Canonical width1, alternate-topology width4 (up to three variants per parent), and hybrid with a projected-incumbent reset available at every depth. Fourteen runs over the flags of MMG-026. Independent carry approaches end at classical rank27 and 64; hybrid ends at rank24 and 47 with integrated profiles 127 and 470. Hybrid is not an improvement over incumbent; it can select a new ground at intermediate depth. The carried spaces generally did not exploit the next query. Search counts are not matched work across policies.

## MMG-028 — exact entangled topology shortcut without a decomposition seed

For a prefix of rank-one queries q_i=u_i v_i^T and their individually minimal rank-n realizations, each retained atom of that query lies in the left column space u_i⊗F^n and right row space v_i⊗F^n. This follows because a rank-n matrix factorization into n terms has independent factors spanning its exact row/column spaces. Suppose the next rank-one query q has a rank-one separator x y^T satisfying x^T q_i y=0 for all previous i and x^T q y=1. Contract the output row index of a residual with x and its output column index with y. This is multiplication of the slice matrix on left and right by fixed matrices, so cannot increase rank. Every retained atom vanishes; M(q) becomes I_n. Therefore every representative in M(q)+K has rank at least n. The ordinary rank-n addition is optimal for this particular carry-forward model. If no such separator exists (call the step entangled), this obstruction is absent; its absence alone does not prove a shortcut.

For n=2 enumerate all nine nonzero rank-one queries and all 1,944 ordered independent rank-one query bases. For each first three queries enumerate the three unordered minimum two-atom factorizations independently (27 topologies), and all 64 linear combinations of the six retained atoms. Test rank of final residual. Total 3,359,232 exact rank tests. Separable last steps: 1,728 flags, 46,656 topologies, no rank-one residual. Entangled last steps: 216 flags, 5,832 topologies, 1,296 successful topology/residual combinations; every entangled flag has a successful combination (six per flag). Each success reconstructs and verifies the entire dense T2 coefficient tensor. First witness:
q=[1,8,15,2], rank-one query masks in row-major order, i.e. C11, C22, sum of all entries, C12. Prefix atoms [[1,1],[2,4],[4,10],[12,8],[10,15],[15,3]]. Residual uses retained mask 41 and new atom [14,11]. The final output masks in original C coordinates are [[1,1,3],[2,4,5],[4,10,12],[12,8,10],[10,15,4],[15,3,2],[14,11,6]]. Prefix cost 2,4,6,7, starting at exact lower bound. No rank7 decomposition is supplied to this synthesis. This recovers known rank7, not a new rank bound. Counts are for GF(2), this specific carry model and ordered query flags.

## MMG-029 — larger entangled flags

Generate six random rank-one query bases and six bases with the first n²−1 queries spanning the trace-zero hyperplane, for each n=3 and n=4. The latter final separator would have to be the full-rank identity matrix, so no rank-one separator exists. Apply the MMG-027 alternate-topology carry algorithm without incumbent injection, and verify every full tensor. All 24 runs end at classical 27 or 64; no shortcut in the bounded residual search. Entanglement therefore acts as a necessary screen for this fixed minimal-prefix model, not a general sufficient criterion or proven predictor of finite-budget search. The exact n=2 result suggests a more targeted joint search over prefix factorization topologies and latent residual rank, rather than committing early to a single factorization. Next: compose entangled subflags across shared dimensions, optimize simultaneous multi-query residuals, and test independent topology choices against source-pinned rank47/95 grounds.
