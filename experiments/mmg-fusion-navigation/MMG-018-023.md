# MMG-018–023 — Exact fitting, pair-span rigidity and adaptive work budgets
## Intention

Retain the formal unrestricted matrix multiplication tensor identity over GF(2). Continue LGFC-001 v0.1 with an explicit separation between exploration (construct or change the supporting ground) and fitting (find a smaller realization inside the accumulated ground). All claimed rank improvements have an independent coefficient verification. Rank is nonscalar products, not total circuit arithmetic. The square target remains r^4<n^9; exponent below two is an intentionally infeasible negative control in the ordinary explicit-output arithmetic model.

## MMG-018: hyperplane fitting

Fix a free leg a of a rank-r decomposition, and suppose its pair atoms on the other two legs form an independent basis of K. Write the coefficient vectors of the target slice space in this basis as C≤GF(2)^r. For nonzero z in C^perp, the hyperplane H_z contains C. Any rank-one atom of K with coordinate vector x satisfying z·x=0 is admissible inside H_z. If such atoms span C, solve the free leg anew. If the span has dimension <r, this is an exact rank reduction. In particular, spanning H_z suffices for reduction by at least one. This is stronger than local duplicate merging. MMG-018 enumerates pair-atom sums of weight 2 or 3, checks rank-one via equal nonzero rows over GF(2), and checks dual relations of weight 2–4; a restricted catalogue. Split rank8 control returns rank7; isolated rank8 and ranks25,47,95 do not improve under this restricted operation. The JSON records each count. This is not a proof of infeasibility outside the tested catalogue.

## MMG-019: full rank-one catalogue by quotient kernels

For each nonzero u∈GF(2)^d, d=n², solve the homogeneous system Q(u⊗v)=0, with Q the quotient by K. Each nullspace element v≠0 gives one rank-one atom in K. Over GF(2) each nonzero rank-one matrix has a unique factorization, so iterating all 2^d−1 left vectors is exhaustive. Gaussian elimination avoids enumerating all possible right vectors. The cost of enumerating the nullspace itself is charged by the resulting catalogue. Enumerate all nonzero dual vectors when dual dimension≤20. Search for a hyperplane whose rank-one atoms span it, reconstruct all free-leg coefficients, and verify every formal tensor coefficient.

Barriers depend on the representation and the operation library.

## MMG-020: Gray-code navigation and published incumbents

Precompute Q(e_i⊗e_j) for all coordinate pairs. As u traverses Gray order, its d projected columns are updated with exactly d XORs per new u; no repeated projection elimination. Still solve each right-factor kernel independently. For n=4 scan all 65,535 nonzero u per leg (3,145,680 kernel columns and the same number of projection-update XORs). Published rank47 yields complete catalogues of sizes 47,48,47. For n=5 scan 16,384 selected left vectors per leg (incumbent left factors, units, pairwise XORs, seeded random additions). Partial catalogues 140,103,141; not exhaustive. No improvement of either published incumbent.

## MMG-021: complete pair-space closure test and rank47 rigidity

Strengthen the hyperplane test: do not require all of H_z to be spanned, merely C⊆span of admissible rank-one atoms. If any decomposition of rank <r is possible using only atoms in K, its atom span is proper and contains C, hence it lies inside some H_z. Complete catalogue and complete dual scan therefore decide whether rank reduction exists within that fixed K. For rank24, all 32,767 nonzero dual vectors on each leg are checked; no proper supporting subspace generated by rank-one atoms contains C. This is an exact closure certificate relative to the three fixed pair spaces, not a global rank lower bound.

For rank47, no full dual scan is needed. With only incumbent atoms available and every free coefficient nonzero, no incumbent atom can be omitted. With just one extra atom available, a smaller decomposition would omit at least two incumbent atoms. In GF(2) each omitted coordinate would receive the same nonzero free-leg coefficient from that one extra atom. All 47 original free-leg coefficients are distinct and nonzero on each leg, contradiction. Thus no smaller decomposition exists in any of the three original pair spaces. This is why fitting these fixed spaces more aggressively cannot help; future exploration must alter the supporting space itself. The certificate is specific to the pinned rank47 witness.

## MMG-023: leave the pair space via a one-dimensional lift

Enumerate all (2^9−1)^2 = 261,121 nonzero rank-one atoms for each leg of the rank24 witness, including atoms outside K. Group by Q(u⊗v). In GF(2) two outside atoms in the same nonzero coset have a difference h in K; it can be realized using two products sharing the same free coefficient. Enumerate all two-atom coset pairs, deduplicate h by exact K coordinate vector. Then choose two distinct dual constraints of weight≤4; the intersection contains C and has codimension two. Build the inside-atom span, and if the residual of C has dimension one, search for a same-coset difference completing it. A successful realization with inside span dimension≤r−3 would cost at most r−1 rank-one terms, including two outside atoms. Exact verification gates admission.

Across three legs: 783,363 atoms catalogued, 57,087 outside-pair comparisons, 2,040 distinct differences (sum across legs), 5,554 dual-pair intersections and three one-dimensional residuals. No rank23 witness. Only this particular two-constraint/two-outside-atom construction is tested; the full lifted search remains open. Larger cosets can supply multiple independent difference directions while paying for one outside anchor; couple that with multi-constraint selection in the next round.

## LGFC refinement and scope

Complexity debt is defined relative to the graph of available transformations. A sufficiently expressive fitting operation may remove an apparent positive barrier entirely, as shown by MMG-019. Exploration should deliberately change supporting spaces when a closure certificate excludes improvement; fitting should occur when the accumulated space is compressible. This motivates LGFC-001 v0.2 as a conditional hypothesis about a jointly adaptive move library and fitting policy, evaluated by charged first-hit work, not a universal dominance claim. The strongest current evidence is exact barrier and closure certificates, not statistical generalization. Full rank and cost targets for unrestricted multiplication remain unmet; rank24 is our improved internal witness only. All experiments executed in the JS tool runtime. Remote fabric execution and CI verification remain outstanding.
