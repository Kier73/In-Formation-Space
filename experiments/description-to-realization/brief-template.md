# Construction brief (experimental template)

**Brief ID / revision:**  
**Date:**  
**Prepared by:**  

## Desired result

Describe the result in ordinary language. Avoid prescribing an implementation unless the implementation is part of the requirement.

## Hard constraints

List conditions that must hold. State how each can be checked.

## Soft preferences

List desirable properties that may be traded off. Do not silently promote these to hard constraints.

## Success criteria and witnesses

What observation, test, proof, or external receipt would count as evidence that the result meets the brief?

## Available resources and boundaries

Tools, runtime, data, materials, permissions, budget, time limits, and prohibited actions.

## Known context

Facts and source artifacts supplied to every independent contributor. Identify provenance where relevant.

## Unknowns and allowed discretion

What is genuinely unknown? Which choices may the constructor make independently, and which require clarification?

## Required return

Ask the constructor to provide:

1. The artifact or explicit no-solution / blocked result.
2. A concise construction trace and exact inputs.
3. Assumptions and choices introduced during construction.
4. Hard-constraint results and soft-preference trade-offs.
5. Tests or witnesses actually produced, with commands and environment where applicable.
6. Remaining unknowns and the next useful observation.

## Evaluator notes

Leave blank until an independent evaluation is performed. Do not let a constructor's success claim substitute for evidence.
