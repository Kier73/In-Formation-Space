## What this result DOES and DOES NOT establish

**Established:** a single canonical signed mathematical intention can be independently realized across two distinct logical/physical host arrangements with precisely retained semantic identities, negative controls for unauthorized invocation, explicitly bounded internal-byte accounting, different receiver execution authority, and an independent GitHub-attested mathematical audit.

**Not established:** physical hardware execution on an actual one- or two-port chip, three physical CPUs, in-wire computation, independent provider or source-origin administrative control, external network data-plane observation, physically meaningful transport timing, distributed exactly-once side effects, arbitrary state-space law navigation, or a VGI certificate for this recurrence.

The publisher signing key was ephemeral and trusted through workflow-attested public-key bootstrap, **not** an independent long-lived publisher credential. GitHub remains the control plane, artifact delivery operator, and attestation issuer. Local elapsed nanoseconds in two runner processes are incomparable clocks; they do not measure inter-machine transport duration.
