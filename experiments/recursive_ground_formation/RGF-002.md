# RGF-002 — Symbolic Ground formation beyond enumerable geometries

Continuation of [RGF-001](README.md). The periodic prefix baseline won on a 257-coordinate carrier. Here the declared state geometry is F_p^n, p=2^61-1 (a known Mersenne prime), n=8,16,32. Each interval evolves x'=x+v, y'=y+f(x); a separately supplied event journal changes v and y at its boundary before the next step. Source f is an explicit bounded sparse monomial description, degree <=8, not an opaque arbitrary oracle. This is a deliberately restricted source language. The complete descriptor is available to verifier and competing routes.

On one interval, g(t)=f(x+t v) has degree at most d. A candidate H in F_p[t] of degree <=d+1 with H(0)=0 is sufficient iff H(t+1)-H(t)=g(t) as polynomials. Because both sides have degree <=d<p, equality at d+1 distinct field points proves the identity. Thus y advances by H(length) and x by length*v, with exact modular arithmetic even for lengths above p. External events split intervals. The source description's structural degree bound is validated; unknown fields and excessive degree refuse. Samples alone cannot prove that bound. The verifier evaluates at 101..101+d using independently implemented grouped exponentiation, not the construction's samples or expansion. This is finite-field algebra, not a claim for arbitrary black-box dynamics.

Five routes: bounded eager (refuses >4096 total steps), cold Newton (degree-specific basis retained within one task), warm Newton (retained across tasks), direct symbolic restriction and descending triangular antidifference with cached Pascal/inverse plan, and a simple fixed hybrid (eager for intervals <=16, direct otherwise). Newton basis formation is a preselected algorithmic family, not autonomous invention; coefficients are discovered per interval by finite differences. Its basis is independently checked at sufficient distinct points. The direct baseline is intentionally strong. It caches generic plans too. Current cold/warm basis builder recomputes the same small inverse for each coefficient in a row; this inefficiency is charged and may inflate cold formation savings. Compare the strong direct baseline and absolute totals, not just warm/cold. No state answers or source coefficients are cached across tasks.

Independent output oracle constructs cumulative values at 0..d+1 and evaluates by Lagrange interpolation, not the Newton or triangular solver. Eager cross-checks all short and medium cases. The huge campaign includes horizons above p and explicit velocity changes. All output state and velocity coordinates are compared. Controls reject changed and oversized certificates, unknown source fields, excessive source degree, duplicate events, and demonstrate missing external event changes the state. Internal journal validity cannot prove completeness.

Work model = charged field arithmetic + integer division in extended Euclid + selected structural visits (source terms, factors and event boundaries). SourceCalls reported separately (not double counted). Modular reduction, coefficient indexing, allocation, validation of individual state/event coordinates, cache lookups, integer/BigInt overhead and wall time are not comprehensively charged; costs are not a machine-speed measure. Audit oracle work separately reported. Packed retained coefficient payload assumes 8 bytes per field element, excludes object overhead; not total memory. Eager refusal costs are partial (validation only), never treated as completed benchmark. GeometryBits describes the ambient x-space, not a proof of orbit size: translation orbit has period dividing p. Enumeration of even a full nonzero-velocity orbit would require p visits. External journals are synthetic, not live E-CPU measurements.
