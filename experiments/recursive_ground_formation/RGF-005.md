## Exact theorem

Over F_p, degree(f)<p, for affine generators F_a(x)=A_a x+b_a, let W_0 be the row span of the coefficient covectors of grad f (RGF-004). Let W_* be the smallest row space containing W_0 and stable under right multiplication by every A_a. Then ker W_* equals the directions u for which f(F_w(x+lambda u))=f(F_w(x)) for all x, lambda and all finite action words w.

Proof: affine maps send translation differences u to A_a u, independent of bias. By RGF-004, a direction invisible to f for all x is exactly ker W_0. If A_w is not surjective, the direct converse for arbitrary affine words can fail: f may only be evaluated on a proper affine image. However the minimal *closed linear processor* theorem does not need surjectivity: any linear B that factors f and admits induced linear/affine transitions BA_a=M_a B must contain W_0 and be closed, hence contains W_*. Conversely the constructed basis satisfies both conditions. For invertible generators, their products are surjective and the all-future translation-direction equality follows as stated. Generated source actions are invertible (nonzero weighted permutations conjugated by an invertible shear); the code supports other affine actions but only claims minimum closed linear rank there. Pairwise future-observation equivalence can be coarser than equality of Bx for nonlinear f; this is not a minimum arbitrary nonlinear machine.

Construct queue of independent covectors; each admitted row contributes one image per generator, at most n additions to the basis. Reduce to pivot chart; q(z)=f(Sz) for a section BS=I. Derived actions T_a and c_a satisfy BA_a=T_a B and Bb_a=c_a. Independent verifier derives raw gradient by multiplicities, saturates with right-pivot elimination and column-index multiplication, checks ranks, exact polynomial substitution, and every induced affine identity.
