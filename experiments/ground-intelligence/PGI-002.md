# PGI-002 — Induced and composable procedural vocabulary

## Architecture

PGI-001 learns an advisory shift ordering and retains independently admitted temporal Grounds. PGI-002 adds a typed callable language of observation pullbacks, a cross-context motif learner and a bounded goal-only search over primitives, retained skills and ordered pairs of skills. The implementation imports the actual PGI-001 Intelligence and CIG-034A/Navigable-Compute Ground kernel. The source-qualified campaign uses the CIG-021/024/034A admission pipeline, not a claim of physical host authority. Cross-repository inspiration: [VL-I PFI-001](https://github.com/Kier73/VL-I/blob/main/docs/PROBLEM_FORMED_INTERPRETER_PFI001.md) derives an interpreter from an observer quotient; [NCR-007](https://github.com/Kier73/Navigable-Compute/blob/main/docs/NCR-007.md) qualifies operator composition; [CIG-032A](../pnp-ground-navigation/cut_intention/CIG-032A.md) separates candidate discovery from exact activation.

## Semantics and qualification

A primitive is `('advance',d)` for d>=0, `('stride',a)` for a>=1, or `('flip',0)`. Each takes the current Boolean temporal function and returns respectively f(t+d), f(a*t), or 1-f(t). For a trace in execution order, normalize to a triple (A,B,C) describing g(t)=f(A*t+B) XOR C. Start at (1,0,0); advance d updates B += A*d; stride a updates A *= a; flip toggles C. Two skills in execution order compose as (A1*A2, B1+A1*B2, C1 XOR C2). `verify_skill` independently traverses the trace backwards, reconstructs the original argument and parity, and compares the exact triple. The operations are restricted to nonnegative time and the inherited 4096-bit parameter/query contract. The semantic identity is on nonnegative mathematical integer times; calls to finite-width source utilities must individually satisfy their own integer contracts.

For a qualified CRT View with finite prefix length D and common period P, the transformed prefix is explicit at 0<=t<max(0,ceil((D-B)/A)). On the periodic tail each original prime-power radix m becomes m/gcd(m,A); arc r selects old arc (A*r+B) mod m. Drop radix 1 and reduce identical branches. Therefore P/gcd(P,A) is a valid common period (not necessarily minimal). The producer visits each old diagram node once; it charges one cell plus each emitted arc and each prefix observation. Costs of hashing, integer arithmetic, node validation and evaluation depth are additional. `verify_pullback` independently traverses a synchronized product of the old and new diagrams across the union of prime coordinates, including unequal powers, and separately checks the entire finite prefix. The verifier charges visited pairs, residue arcs and prefix observations; it does not enumerate the global period. Worst-case pair expansion can exceed a declared budget. `False` from the checker conflates inconsistency and budget refusal; search exhaustion is UNKNOWN, not an impossibility theorem. Only verified candidates are returned.

## Induction and activation

`Vocabulary.observe(source, trace, target)` independently checks the complete trace normal form against the supplied target. It gathers contiguous subsequences of length 2..max_span (default 6) and the whole trace when longer. It deduplicates by exact normal form, excludes trivial identities and existing search primitives, and scores support across distinct registered view identities. This is support over supplied demonstration identities, not an assertion of statistical independence. Repeating one source identity cannot increase support. A fixed bounded capacity retains the highest support*(trace length-1) candidates; all selected trace identities are independently checked. Previously admitted skills survive subsequent induction if capacity allows. The initial primitive search palette is advance1/2, stride2/3, flip; depth is bounded and equivalent triples are deduplicated. Skill semantics are universal over admitted mathematical Views; candidate selection is empirical. Training uses supplied verified procedures, not autonomous discovery of the primitive algebra itself.

`discover` receives a source and target but no construction trace. It tests retained forms, optionally ordered pairs of skills, then the bounded primitive grammar. `primitive_first` tries the cheap primitives first. All candidates have an independent whole-domain proof before return. A pair can be learned by observing its checked discovery in several contexts and inducing again; this is new callable vocabulary but not a new mathematical operator family. Learned pair search remains quadratic in the offered skill count and can be inferior to another discovery method. The primitive baseline uses the same normal-form deduplication and semantic checker; its horizon is five primitives, not a proof that larger or alternative grammars are incapable. If a trace is already known, ordinary algebraic fusion achieves the same direct compiler speed without training.

`SkillIntelligence` integrates the actual PGI-001 controller, constructs a new View from a known source and learned skill, independently qualifies it, and registers it as reusable memory. Selection and typed expression are separately checked. A valid derived Ground may remain even if selection exhausts its budget. `verify_skill_expression` binds the requested source, skill, ordinal, destination and derived target, independently checks pullback and exact ordinal selection. All output and checkpoint claims concern mathematical Views; an external source adapter must qualify the underlying source. A restored vocabulary packet independently rechecks every skill's trace, normal form and digest. It retains callable skills only (session-specific support histories are not serialized); admission does not depend on support scores. A PGI-001 checkpoint and a vocabulary packet can be restored together, as exercised in tests. No distributed authentication or remote energy measurement is claimed.

```python
from skill_language import Vocabulary, SkillIntelligence
from verify_skill_language import verify_skill_expression
from ground_intelligence import Intelligence
# source/target are actual admitted mathematical Views; trace is an observed procedure
v = Vocabulary()
for source, trace, target in demonstrations:
    assert v.observe(source, trace, target)
v.induce()
skill, profile = v.discover(new_source, new_target, compose_skills=True)
assert skill is not None
# after the skill is retained by induction:
agent = Intelligence(); key = agent.observe(new_source, memory=True)
packet, costs = SkillIntelligence(agent, v).express(key, retained_skill.key, 10**900+17)
assert verify_skill_expression(agent.graph, v, key, retained_skill.key, 10**900+17, packet)
```

## Measured direct usage

36 synthetic training episodes on distinct random CRT Boolean Views give 25 skills and a 2,971-byte vocabulary packet (training 25.34 ms including generation, sequential execution, qualification and induction). 135 held-out tasks evaluated by three frozen policies, giving 360 verified discoveries and 45 UNKNOWN controls. All definitive relations checked by the independent product verifier; separate randomized explicit trajectory tests use 300 seeded laws/traces. Matched 60 tasks: primitive 9,860 proposals / 1,444,779 charged verifier cells / 285.26 ms; learned 140 / 24,441 / 5.44 ms; primitive-first hybrid 440 / 29,671 / 7.02 ms. Known trace fusion reduces charged construction+qualification from 105,200 to 22,360 across those 60 tasks (not a learning-only benefit). Simple primitive tasks favor the primitive or hybrid route (30 proposals vs 780 for learned first); novel tasks have approximately equal costs; constant-target refusal remains UNKNOWN on all policies. Timing excludes target generation and includes repeated independent checks for successful candidates; charged discovery work counts only the first candidate-check calls. It is not physical energy or all CPU work.

New procedure: training motifs with normal forms (6,12,1) and (9,5,1) compose to (54,42,0). The 10-instruction demonstration recipe was withheld during training; the normalized form was absent from memory. At depth 5 both noncomposing controls resolve 0/12, after 5,304 proposals each. Pair composition resolves 12/12 in 384 proposals / 38,387 charged cells / 5.93 ms. On subsequent use, acquire the checked trace on 12 independent source identities, reinduce, and retain 35 skills. A further held-out instance is solved on proposal 1 (172 cells). This is a growth in the offered language under a bounded search contract; wider direct normal-form or algebraic search could also discover this form.

The source-qualified campaign uses actual nonlinear CIG marker transport, source CNF proof and a rare-disjunction observation. It independently constructs the four-step target (2,9,0), performs goal-only search, then calls the skill on the original source without supplying a target. The grown 35-skill vocabulary needs 19 proposals on each source (ordering changed after new acquisition). Primitive baseline needs 44/44/72/44. In three cases (2,8,0) is also an exact equivalent relation on the source rare disjunction; both are independently verified, so historical trace recovery is not required. For each of 60/174/384/1023 observation bits, 8 ordinals near 10^900 are checked against independent congruence solutions and original CIG-021 terminal receipts at A*t+B. Largest source P=614889782588491410, compact P'=307444891294245705. Source setup 729.26 ms; 19 learned proposals/2282 cells/0.38 ms versus 44 primitive/23105 cells/3.65 ms; 8 callable realizations including compilation, independent expression verification, separate residue and terminal checks take 30.70 ms. These are goal-discovery timings, not a claim of end-to-end speedup versus fresh source admission. All raw results are in PGI-002-results.json.
