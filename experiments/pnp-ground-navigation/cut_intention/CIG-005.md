## 1. Research question and answer

**Can several future-observation intentions form one shared minimal computational language, and can a nonlinear observer decoder require fewer bits than the minimum *linear* feature representation?**

For the bounded CNF source here, **yes, in two distinct, precisely audited senses**:

1. Over all future times, three separately generated minimum linear pullback-invariant spaces have dimensions **4, 3 and 4**, summing to **11**. Their **shared** minimum pullback-invariant linear span has dimension **9**. Two redundant features disappear because the observers share part of the same future computational structure.
2. When the declared intention is limited to observations **at times `t >= 3`**, three original eventual readout functions have **GF(2) linear dimension 3**, but their realized image consists of exactly **four** observation triples. Thus the minimum lossless binary **nonlinear** observer coordinate has **two bits**. The missing third output can be reconstructed as a Boolean product of the other two.

These are exact mathematical results *for the discovered source-derived transition family*. They do not assume that nonlinear language compilation is polynomial-time on arbitrary SAT, nor assert a P-versus-NP resolution.

## 2. The same original CNF, three observers and one chosen processor

`make_branching_source()` emits an ordinary CNF with **90 original endpoint variables and 106 original clauses**. The 90 equality clauses from CIG-001's complete-graph labels give 45 independent edge bits. Sixteen additional three-literal clauses constrain seven active labels, without passing opcode names to the compiler. CIG-003's `source_reduce` obtains the seven-label CNF after the original equality copying is independently replayed.

Local complete truth-table qualification (the actual CIG-004 `_discover`) finds four directed Boolean relations. The **declared ascending-variable orientation** yields a synchronous repair transformation, with protected `a,b,c`:

\[
\begin{aligned}
a'&=a,&b'&=b,&c'&=c,\\
h'&=ab, &j'&=h\oplus c,\\
y'&=j\oplus c,&z'&=hj.
\end{aligned}
\]

The three declared observer readouts are `O=(y,j,z)`. `j` and `y` share one temporal backbone, and `z` branches nonlinearly from `h,j`. Every source clause is assigned to exactly one local total Boolean relation, and the source is independently audited to satisfy:

\[
F_{\mathrm{active}}(x)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad T(x)=x.
\]

The original CNF specifies those **fixed points**. On a non-fixed initial vector, `T` is a *chosen* source-equivalent repair dynamics; original Boolean relations alone do not force this unique temporal interpretation. That orientation is retained in the receipt. The free original-label dimensions are `45 - 4 = 41`, giving

\[
\boxed{\#SAT(F_{\mathrm{original}})=2^{41}}
\]

with an independently original-clause-checked satisfying witness. The receiver also enumerates all 128 active states as a bounded oracle and confirms that exactly eight are fixed points.

## 3. Constructing the shared full-future space

Write Boolean functions in algebraic normal form (ANF), with addition `\oplus` and multiplication in the idempotent Boolean ring

\[
\mathbb F_2[x_0,\dots,x_6]/(x_i^2-x_i).
\]

A monomial is represented by a bitmask of its variable support. Exact multiplication uses **support union** and coefficient parity; this preserves the key CIG-004 and NCR-007 rule `x_i²=x_i`. For the chosen transition, define the pullback `Kf=f\circ T`.

For a set of observers `\mathcal O`, construct the smallest linear subspace of exact source functions that contains the observers and remains stable under every application of `K`:

\[
\boxed{W(\mathcal O)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_2}\{K^tO_i:t\ge0,\ O_i\in\mathcal O\}.}
\]

This is *minimal in the class of GF(2)-linear, pullback-invariant ANF feature spaces preserving every requested temporal readout*. It is not a claim of minimum arbitrary nonlinear circuit size or minimal physical measurement cost.

The producer computes closure by bounded symbolic substitution and exact GF(2) elimination. A separate verifier rebuilds the original CNF function tables, independently constructs every requested orbit, proves equality of generated and reconstructed feature spans, checks each feature-transition matrix row and confirms the observer decoding masks. This construction has **9** independent shared features, instead of **11** when the three individual spans are stored independently.

The retained feature transition matrix supports temporal navigation through binary exponentiation. For the experiment's `t=10^100+173`, the matrix method performs **442** small matrix-composition operations. The receiver independently recomputes the composition and additionally compares exact nonlinear source stepping against matrix output for every active state at finite times `t=0..6`. An incorrect matrix multiplication orientation is therefore not silently accepted.

## 4. Switching intention: eventual observations and nonlinear quotient

Symbolic ANF pullback proves first stabilization at `t=3`, with

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
Y&=y\circ T^3=ab,\\
J&=j\circ T^3=ab\oplus c,\\
Z&=z\circ T^3=ab\oplus abc.
\end{aligned}}
\]

Each polynomial is invariant under one more pullback, `K Y=Y`, `K J=J`, `K Z=Z`. Consequently the same formulas hold for **all** initial configurations and **every** `t >= 3`, without stepping `t` times.

The three ANFs are **linearly independent** over `GF(2)` (their span dimension is 3). But their realized output image has only four states. The nonlinear identity is

\[
\boxed{Z=YJ,\qquad Y^2=Y.}
\]

Indeed `(ab)(ab\oplus c)=ab\oplus abc`. Thus `(Y,J)` is a sufficient two-bit exact observer for the full output triple `(Y,J,Z)`, with decoder `Z=Y\land J`.

An even smaller binary encoding would require fewer than two bits, and could represent at most two distinct output values. Four source-realized values are independently exhibited, so the **two-bit** minimum is rigorous among *all lossless binary encodings of the eventual observer image*, not just linear features.

| Output triple `(Y,J,Z)` | Active preserved `(a,b,c)` assignments | Count over 128 possible initial active states |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `(0,0,0)` | 3 | 48 |
| `(1,0,0)` | 1 | 16 |
| `(0,1,0)` | 3 | 48 |
| `(1,1,1)` | 1 | 16 |

The producer enumerates only **eight** assignments to the actual support variables `(a,b,c)` to construct this image; all other initial active coordinates are irrelevant to these eventual readouts. The independent receiver additionally checks all **128** original active configurations. This is finite support enumeration under a measured budget, not a universal shortcut for unstructured Boolean systems.

This is a concrete example of the distinction highlighted in NCR-007 between **minimum invariant linear feature dimension** and **realized observer quotient cardinality**.

## 5. Source-derived observation offers and a nonlinear decoder

The next question is operational: *Which observations should we ask an available measurement path to provide?*

The offered mathematical readings are the original three outputs and two exact composites `y XOR j` and `y AND j`. The observer policy receives **declared integer costs**, not externally attested sensor prices:

| Offered readout | Declared acquisition cost |
| --- | ---: |
| `y` | 3 |
| `j` | 2 |
| `z` | 6 |
| `y XOR j` | 1 |
| `y AND j` | 2 |

For every subset of the five offered readings (31 nonempty sets), the compiler tests whether the measured outcome uniquely determines the triple `(Y,J,Z)` on every possible source-supported future observation. It accounts for all attempted 220 local state evaluations, counts admissible policies, and minimizes total declared cost with deterministic tie breaking. The independent receiver redoes this optimization and **requires that the price schedule be provided by the receiver's intention**, rather than trusting numbers written into the produced certificate.

The optimal readouts are:

\[
\boxed{P_0=J,\qquad P_1=Y\oplus J=c,\qquad \mathrm{cost}=2+1=3.}
\]

Because `Y=P_0\oplus P_1`, the full nonlinear decoder is

\[
\boxed{(Y,J,Z)=(P_0\oplus P_1,\ P_0,\ (P_0\oplus P_1)P_0).}
\]

Its truth-table outputs, ordered by the two-bit observed integer, are `[0,7,1,2]` (integer bit order `Y` then `J` then `Z`). The receiver checks this exact ANF identity by substituting the source-derived offered-polynomials into each decoded bit. Thus the check is algebraic, not a fitted lookup on a handful of observed examples.

The naïve policy of buying all three raw readouts costs `3+2+6=11` declared units; selecting the two derived observers costs 3. This is an **optimization of an explicitly offered mathematical contract**. It does not assert a physical sensor can obtain a composite parity for one unit, or that all real costs of constructing an acquisition path are included. When the price of composite observations is raised to 999, the exact policy instead selects `y,j` for declared cost 5. Under an alternative zero-price raw contract it selects those two for cost 0. No measurement is silently acquired; no unauthenticated observation becomes Ground truth.

## 7. What to test next — CIG-006

The resulting new mathematical direction is more specific than simply shrinking a state vector:

1. **Generate observer candidate languages from arbitrary source constraints**, not a fixture with a known acyclic orientation. Enumerate and certify all admissible local interpretations where several are possible, and preserve interpretation identity through every revision.
2. **Discover nonlinear decoders automatically** rather than supplying only simple AND/XOR offers. Use bounded ANF synthesis or truth-table minimization with independent source equality proof and explicit model/image construction cost.
3. **Extend from eventual fixed-point dynamics to cycles and event-triggered intentions.** Replace `K^h O=K^(h+1) O` with a verified ultimately periodic observer module or minimal polynomial representation, and account for preperiod and period.
4. **Optimize for physical observation policies only after observation costs and path authority are proven.** Connect verified VGI probe outcomes and VL-I/E-CPU host claims, preserving mathematical source identity separately from measurement and execution authority.
5. **Challenge the compiler on adversarial or held-out CNFs.** Compare the *full* source→formation→navigation→decoder→independent-check cost against PNG, SFI, SPN and CDCL; retain `UNKNOWN` when bounded construction has no complete proof.

The P-versus-NP investigation benefits from exact distinctions: a problem's compact output, a minimal linear feature space, a minimal nonlinear observer quotient, and the cost of *finding* each Ground are different complexity resources. CIG-005 supplies a source-bound instance where all four are measured or certified separately.
