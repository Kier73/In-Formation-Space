## Exact lifted time operator

The static decoder source C(x) and observations q(x) establish G(q(x))=f(x) on source-valid states. A separate explicitly supplied CNF L(u,v) states a temporal transition on k observed coordinates. A declared contiguous partition into blocks of w bits is used (1<=w<=4, k<=4096, k divisible by w). The compiler discovers predecessor blocks and local functions from L, requiring every normalized clause to own exactly one next block, refer to only one old block per region, and the complete local relation to have exactly one output for every input. Predecessor indices must form a permutation. The independent verifier exhausts each local 2w-bit relation with tuple assignments and checks full clause ownership. Non-total, nondeterministic, fanout and cross-block source contracts are refused.

With m=k/w and alphabet A={0,...,2^w-1}, write T(u)_j=F_j(u_{p(j)}). The map p is a spatial permutation, but F_j may be nonlinear and noninjective. A moving symbol has lifted vertex (i,a), with successor E(i,a)=(p^{-1}(i), F_{p^{-1}(i)}(a)). Each initial block symbol follows exactly one deterministic path; at every t the destination locations form a permutation. Inductively, the m symbols reconstruct T^t(u).

The complete lifted graph contains m*2^w vertices instead of 2^k observation codes. Record depth d to a directed cycle and entry e. The independent proof checks every cycle edge, unique cycle membership, d=0 exactly for cycle vertices, and d(v)=d(E(v))+1, e(v)=e(E(v)) otherwise. These equations force the actual transient length and entry. For t>=d, the jump is a cycle lookup at (pos(e)+t-d) mod cycle length. For t<d, the producer uses binary lifting on transient paths and the receiver walks the bounded transient. The number of long-time symbol lookups is m, independent of the magnitude of t; integer arithmetic, packing, decoder evaluation and proof admission are separately charged. Certificate size is O(m*2^w) integers plus local tables. Transient powers occupy O(m*2^w log D) space.

The temporal law is separate from the static source; no physical dynamics or unique microscopic trajectory is inferred. The pure receiver binds the initial code and logical time, verifies the shared source proof and transition, and checks the time-jumped code and outputs.

## Source-image qualification

Without a total-image proof, initial and terminal source witnesses are mandatory. This qualifies endpoints only, not intermediate codes. The new constructive section proves exists x: C(x) and q(x)=u for every u.

For disjoint nonempty signed observation clauses, choose one literal equal to its observation bit and all remaining literals false. Use the actual CIG-017 local circuit discovery routine to fill the other variables through total local relations. The independent checker checks a topological variable assignment, local satisfaction for every input combination, and complete original normalized clause coverage. Uncovered clauses and overlapping seed predicates refuse admission. Unconstrained variables can be assigned zero. This constructs a section s with C(s(u)) and q(s(u))=u. It establishes closure of the entire observation image under the admitted total T, allowing witness-free temporal receipts. This is a sufficient source-specific surjectivity certificate, not a general surjectivity decision procedure.

Enumerate each constrained local period and derive allowed time residues. Join by generalized CRT: r mod M and s mod L are compatible iff r=s mod gcd(M,L). A complete empty join proves unreachability; otherwise choose the least representative >=max(start,D). Local/CRT budget exhaustion is UNKNOWN. The independent checker rederives all local residues using cycle-position lookup rather than producer simulation, verifies the join and minimal time, and checks the final code.
