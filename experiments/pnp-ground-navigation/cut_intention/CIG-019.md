## Construction

Let P = C(x) AND C(y) AND (q(x)=q(y)). For goal f_i the obligation is P AND f_i(x) AND NOT f_i(y). Construct a colored resolution prefix from P alone, preserving every private copy of any variable mentioned by any declared goal. Shared observation variables may be resolved out too. The partial interpolants still refer to the original shared observation labels. Resolution is sound regardless of elimination order; a complete Davis-Putnam elimination on a variable absent from the goal assumptions preserves existential satisfiability. The scope-aware heap chooses narrow incident scopes, then pair count; stop the shared prefix before the next incident scope exceeds eight. Each suffix uses the residual clauses and its own goal assumptions. Reverse elimination reconstructs counterexample states. Per-prefix and per-suffix pair/step/width budgets apply separately; UNKNOWN is not a proof. Batch status COMPLETE means processing completed, not that every goal was resolved. Prefix budget refusal is UNKNOWN. The catalog is declared in advance, maximum 128 distinct targets; arbitrary online new goals require a compatible retained prefix or new construction (future work).

Every prefix derived clause names two earlier original/derived premises and a pivot. Each suffix has its own index space extending the common prefix with its own goal assumptions. No suffix can reference another suffix. The independent checker rebuilds colored premises from the original source, checks every resolution and the empty root, or checks the paired original-source witnesses directly. Prefix active indices are search hints, not evidence of residual equivalence; proof soundness depends on replaying actual premises, not trusting that residual. Search counters are diagnostics. Source contract binds n, canonical source, ordered q and ordered targets. Proof admission never treats an UNKNOWN goal as sufficient. The proof uses the CIG-018 partial-interpolant rule; A-private pivot OR, other pivot AND. A shared gate interner folds constants, equal and complementary operands, and dual negative-input gates by De Morgan. Only ancestors of successful outputs survive the compact circuit. The receiver independently builds the interner and uses reverse topological liveness. Values outside the realized observation image have no original-source claim.
