**Checkpoint:** 23 November 2026. **Predecessors:** CIG-008/009/010. **Local evidence:** 171 tests, including 14 new methods. ## Mathematical contract

A tree partitions canonical CNF clauses into leaf regions, possibly sharing coordinates; every declared coordinate, including an isolated free coordinate, must be covered. For a subtree R, let `V_R` be its union of leaf scopes, and `S_R = V_R ∩ V_outside`. Its exact conditional count is `M_R(s)=Σ_h [F_R(s,h)]`. For children A,B of R, multiply their conditional counts over their shared coordinates and sum over `S_A ∪ S_B` restricted to the requested `S_R`. This gives the original-source count at the root and supports witness reconstruction.

The cold producer-cell cost of a join is `2^|S_A∪S_B|`. For a leaf it is `2^|scope|` for the Boolean route, and `2^|boundary|` for the recognized complete affine route. This counts table evaluations, not integer bit operations, GF(2) extraction, independent checking, memory, serialization or wall time. Receiver-owned reuse can reduce actual cells below the cold estimate.

For a fixed ordered leaf partition, boundaries of each interval are independent of its binary association. The optimal ordered binary contraction cost satisfies `D(i,j)=min_k(D(i,k)+D(k,j)+2^|S_(i,k) ∪ S_(k,j)|)` over qualified splits. Fixed leaves supply base costs. Ties prefer smaller depth. The finite interval DP has O(w^3) split attempts for a window of w leaves, plus bitset and source costs. For larger leaf sets, recursively optimize contiguous windows, default w=16, max 32. This is a hierarchical heuristic outside a single window, not a global treewidth optimizer. The 12-bit join admission limit and existing 10-coordinate leaf limit are enforced. The independent `audit` checks complete clause ownership, coordinate coverage, and boundary sets before score/realization. Budget refusals remain UNKNOWN.

`select_widths` compares original biconnected discovery, BFS, stack DFS and low-degree-first true DFS regional packing, balanced and optimized trees, and optionally a previously supplied plan for the same source. Default finite leaf-budget catalog (3,8). Candidate formation and scoring are charged in selection time. It chooses the minimum lexicographic `(cells + lambda*nodes, cells, depth, width, nodes, ...)` within the admitted catalog. Lambda is a declared integer cost in cell-equivalent units per regional message/node, not measured network latency. `pareto` emits nondominated (cells,nodes) pairs. This is a cold proxy; warm transition cost must be measured separately.

## Heavy connected cyclic workload

The fixture has k ring coordinates x and k private z, randomized variable labels and clause order. Even edges enforce `z_i=x_i XOR x_(i+1)`, odd edges `z_i=x_i AND x_(i+1)`; every z is fixed zero. The independent fixture oracle computes `trace(Π A_i)` with A_even identity and A_odd=[[1,1],[1,0]]. This is equivalent to the Lucas number L_(k/2) for even k. The compiler receives only original CNF, not the ring or transfer matrices. Every fresh or switched architecture reconstructs a source-valid witness and agrees with the oracle. CIG-010's original biconnected discovery refuses these instances, while the new graph-ordered leaf packing admits them. Dense random controls 50/100/200/500 variables remain UNKNOWN.

## Price-conditioned architecture

At 8192 variables the finite catalog's Pareto frontier is `[[57332, 8191], [278532, 2731]]`. For lambda=0 choose fine; at lambda=64 choose walk-optimized with leaf budget 8, 278532 cells and 2731 nodes. The break-even price is (278532−57332)/(8191−2731)=40.5128 cell-equivalent units per message. No wall-time optimality follows. Architecture formation remains costly; the current model does not account for all receiver/transport costs.
