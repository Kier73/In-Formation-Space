These results establish independent **application-level mathematical

the transfer medium. A GitHub-hosted runner job is a distinct execution
environment, not guaranteed to be a separate physical machine.

this SNC-004 capsule; Tailscale overlay reachability; independent
administrative source provenance; issuer signing; GPU or numerical
floating-point behavior; federated consensus; VGI Ground admission;
or externally effectful E-CPU execution authority. A successful
artifact audit must never be described as a successful overlay hop.

For a future live overlay test, enable dedicated workload-identity
federation and per-host certificate attestation with fail-closed
separate-source authorization, not a public-IP fallback.
