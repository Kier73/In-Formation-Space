It establishes actual TLS socket transport and the complete receiver
mathematical admission protocol on loopback, including independent
test self-signed peer identities. It does not establish a successful
Tailscale transport between independent runner jobs.

application response, and be checked by a third runner before a live

artifact are test bootstrap identities, not external trusted issuers.

execution authority has been demonstrated.
