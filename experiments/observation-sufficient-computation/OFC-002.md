# OFC-002 — The computation succeeds while its original law remains unknown

**Second native milestone:** extend [OFC-001](README.md) from hidden
initial-state uncertainty to hidden **source-law identity**, without adding a
new policy solver, authority, interpreter, or simulated "remote intelligence".

The implemented extension invokes the **original IWI-002**
[`uncertain_ground.compile`](../intention-without-identification/uncertain_ground.js).
It constructs the disjoint law-state product of candidate static Grounds,
then directly reuses IWI-001 `solve/audit` and OFC-001's existing VGI,
Navigable-Compute, E-CPU, and VLD path unchanged.

## Semantics: neither identify nor switch laws

Let candidate laws be `L_alpha` and `L_beta`, each with its own
finite state-space. An actual source starts in exactly one pair
`(L_i, s_i)`. Every `a` maps

\[
(L_i,s) \xrightarrow{a} (L_i,T^i_a(s))
\]

and never changes the `L_i` coordinate. The observer knows that either law
could be true. Its complete alternative set is represented as a disjoint
union, with original candidate identity preserved by all operators.

A guaranteed policy must satisfy the intention under **every** candidate
law and **every** possible initial state; no majority voting or hidden
model-selection oracle is allowed.

## Native paths connected, directly

1. **IFS IWI-002** forms the actual union of candidate Grounds. **IFS
   IWI-001** produces and audits the universal contingent policy, including
   a losing certificate when none exists.
2. **VGI** reconstructs the complete declared source model with explicit
   `known` transitions. Its original `step` method is an independent
   reference for every candidate state and action.
3. **Navigable-Compute NCR-004/005** derives the minimum future-observer
   quotient within its declared model, constructs an observer-native
   algebra, and independently checks every action without relying on
   the IWI policy's chosen trajectories.
4. **E-CPU INTENT-001 + SAB-001** forms a processor whose identity is
   independent of which candidate happens to be true. The receiver-held
   host secretly owns the true law-state coordinate; each action selects
   the cheapest currently authorized available execution path.
5. **Virtual-Layer-Dynamics** receives only exactly verified modular-affine
   action maps as alternative numerical operators when the complete
   union map is affine. Otherwise the actual E-CPU processor is used
   without pretending the map has an unsupported algebraic simplification.

## Three controlled experiments

| Hidden law test | Verified outcome | What the observer learned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `safe_diagnosis` | **Two actions**: safe inspect, then law-specific action; rehost after inspection | The inspection output distinguishes alpha/beta, and prevents an otherwise dangerous speculative intervention |
| `silent_conflict` | **Zero actions**, guaranteed-goal refusal | The candidate laws are individually solvable, but no observation reveals which mutually destructive action to choose |
| `silent_common_funnel` | **One action**, goal for both candidates | Both original laws remain consistent with the final observation and both remain represented in the successful final belief |

The strongest result is `silent_common_funnel`:
- Both candidate models begin in different hidden model-state pairs.
- Both reach their own goal under the same action.
- Their observable output is the same.
- The post-action belief contains **two states belonging to distinct candidate
  laws**, not a single identified model.
- The intention is nevertheless fulfilled.

This is **constructive goal sufficiency without original law
identification**, not merely an example of hiding the starting register.

## Host recovery and falsification

The `safe_diagnosis` run deliberately makes `worker.alpha` disappear
between the two actions. E-CPU's planner uses `worker.beta` without
changing the formed processor fingerprint. There is no re-identification of
the source, or implicit transfer of the source's alleged authority to the
replacement host.

The regression suite also verifies every candidate remains within its own
model component for every action, every state/action interpretation agrees
between VGI/NCR/E-CPU, and a counterfeit self-consistently rehashed
"winning" policy for the losing candidate pair is refused.

### Admission scope and next development

The candidate laws are **declared static, complete synthetic Grounds**. The
IWI-002 product is a mathematical possibility model, not a discovery or
verification of a physical unknown environment. The host's hidden
identity is genuinely withheld from the controller during the run, while
the source model is supplied as the possibility envelope.

The next experiment replaces that assumed envelope with an *evidence-formed*
possibility envelope: VGI policy-finalized partial transition evidence,
IWI-003 unknown cells and IWI-004 active acquisition, qualified
by E-CPU's original source identity/authority contracts. It should
then add only the observations necessary to obtain a guaranteed policy, not
learn all source laws in advance.
