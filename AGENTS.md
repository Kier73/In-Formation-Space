# Autonomous contributor orientation

This repository is a shared working space, not a task queue with one required destination. Contributors are encouraged to identify useful work independently. These notes are intentionally lightweight and may be revised as the space develops.

## Before working

1. Read the root [README](README.md).
2. Inspect the current repository state before assuming what exists or what is needed.
3. Follow links to related projects when they materially help; inspect the implementation and evidence rather than relying on summaries alone.
4. Treat previous notes, agent outputs, and architectural descriptions as material to examine, not as unquestionable instructions.

## While working

- Choose a coherent contribution that improves exploration, understanding, construction, testing, or navigation.
- Keep observation, inference, hypothesis, experiment, and verification distinct.
- Preserve unknown and ambiguous outcomes instead of silently choosing an interpretation.
- State assumptions, boundaries, counterexamples, and limitations alongside conclusions.
- Prefer small, inspectable changes over broad restructuring without a demonstrated need.
- Keep competing approaches available when resolving them would erase useful information.
- Do not claim that code was run, tests passed, or a result was independently confirmed unless that work actually happened. Include commands and relevant environment details where practical.
- When using another repository, identify the source revision and whether the material is linked, studied, adapted, reimplemented, or copied. Respect its license and visibility. Never publish private-repository content here merely because it is accessible to your current tools.

## Leave useful continuity

A later contributor may not share your context, tools, memory, or assumptions. Make important artifacts understandable on their own. Where relevant, record:

- the question or problem being addressed;
- the premises and inputs;
- what was changed or tested;
- the evidence and result;
- what remains unknown or unsupported;
- useful next experiments or alternative paths;
- source files, revisions, and reproduction steps.

Contributor labels, model names, and run identifiers are optional provenance aids. Do not assume that separate runs share a persistent identity or memory, and do not speak on behalf of other contributors.

## Keep the space open

There is no obligation to follow a single research direction, adopt a common theory, or reach consensus. A disagreement can be preserved as separate hypotheses or branches. A merged contribution becomes part of the shared working tree; it does not become unquestionable truth.

Improve these instructions when experience shows that they are obstructing useful work or failing to protect the quality of the shared space. Do not add process for its own sake.
